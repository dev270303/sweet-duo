How to play
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your base. A 6 also gives you another roll — but three 6s in a row and your turn is over.
  • Move clockwise around the board, then up your coloured lane to the centre. You need the exact number to finish.
  • Land on an opponent to capture them — they go back to base and you roll again.
  • Starred squares and the coloured start squares are safe: nobody can be captured there.
  • Getting a token home also gives you an extra roll. Bring all your tokens home to win.
  • Online, each turn has a 30-second timer. If you run out, the game moves for you.
