How to play
  • Take turns rolling the dice and moving your token up the board, 1 → 100.
  • Land at the foot of a ladder and climb it. Land on a snake's head and slide down to its tail.
  • First to reach 100 wins. With Exact roll, a roll that's too high means you stay put; with Bounce back you count back from 100.
  • Party mode adds power-up squares: 🛡️ Shield blocks your next snake, ⚡ Boost jumps 5 squares, 🔄 Swap trades places with the leader, 🎲 Extra gives another roll.
  • Surprise me builds a brand-new board every game.
