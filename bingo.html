BINGO

Two players, two phones, no sign-up.

or join a friend's game

How to play
  1. Each player gets their own 5×5 card with 1–25 shuffled. Shuffle until you like it, then tap Ready.
  2. Take turns picking a number from your card. It gets cut on both cards.
  3. Every full line you complete (row, column or diagonal) cuts one letter of B‑I‑N‑G‑O.
  4. First to cut all five letters wins. If both finish on the same number, it's a draw.
