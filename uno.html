Last Card

Match colours, stack the pressure, shout before you're caught.

or join a friend

How to play
  • Everyone starts with 7 cards. Match the top card by colour or number/symbol.
  • Skip and Reverse give you another turn. +2 makes your friend draw 2 and lose their turn.
  • Wild lets you pick the colour. Wild +4 also makes them draw 4 — allowed only when you have no card of the current colour.
  • Can't play? Tap the deck to draw. If the drawn card fits, you can play it or keep it.
  • Going down to one card? Tap LAST CARD! first. Forget, and your friend can catch you for +2.
  • First to empty their hand wins the round and scores the cards left in the other hand.
