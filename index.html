How to play with a friend

  1. Open a game and tap Create a game.
  2. Share the link or 5-letter code with your friend.
  3. They open the link (or enter the code) and you're connected.
Games connect phone-to-phone. If it won't connect, try switching one phone between Wi‑Fi and mobile data.
